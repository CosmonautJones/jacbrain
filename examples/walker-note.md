# Traversing offers with Jac

A walker separates traversal behavior from graph data. An Offer node can hold
the title; a FindOffers walker visits connected nodes and reports their titles.
The paired example is a compilation candidate until checked by Jac MCP.

Scope: Jac 0.37.23. Consult the installed `jac guide jac-walker-patterns` for
the authoritative guide. This original note is a small demo, not a copied
language manual or evidence of application correctness.
